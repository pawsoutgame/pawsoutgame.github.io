Paws Out – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Paws Out ("the Game", "we") uses and why. Contact: pawsout.support@gmail.com

1. No account, no personal profile

You can play without creating an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

2. Game progress

Your progress (levels, coins, cats, settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS / Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device type, operating system and how you interact with ads.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iOS you are asked for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, your advertising identifier is not shared and you see non-personalised ads.
  • You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's settings.

More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases – Apple, Google and RevenueCat

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. To deliver and restore purchases we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and the details of your purchase transactions. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data and to object to processing. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests are handled by Google (ads) and RevenueCat (purchases) through the links above; you can also contact us and we will assist.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
